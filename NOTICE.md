# Important Notice

This project is a product-naming and regulatory pre-screening prototype for cosmetics marketed in Mainland China.

It is **not legal advice** and does not replace:
- qualified regulatory review;
- filing / registration review;
- trademark clearance;
- formula or efficacy evidence review.

The bundled regulatory data is a versioned reference snapshot. Check `data/sources.json` and the official source URLs before relying on a current regulatory conclusion.

The Interactive Naming Brief Builder is static. By default it has no API, backend, analytics, or server-side storage.
